# License Maintenance Checklist

NexOra PANC v1.1 becomes active when its root LICENSE is merged into the Official Repository.

- Preserve the previous proprietary license unchanged in `docs/licensing-archive/`.
- Keep README, CONTRIBUTING, package metadata, and supporting licensing documents consistent.
- Review historical contributions and confirm authority to license every covered first-party file.
- Audit dependencies, certificates, fonts, icons, images, and other assets for provenance and required notices.
- Do not include secrets, production keys, credentials, or personal data in the repository.
- Obtain explicit contributor consent before accepting contributions under the public license.
- Obtain qualified legal advice for jurisdiction-specific interpretation and commercial agreements.
- Version and document any future license change; do not silently alter rights already granted.

This checklist is guidance only. The root LICENSE controls.
